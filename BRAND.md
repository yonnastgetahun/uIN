# uIN — Brand System

## Name
**uIN** — coined from "flock." Flamingos flock together. So do your friends.

## Tagline System
Use all three in context:

| Tagline | Usage | Energy |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **RSVP Together** | App Store subtitle, onboarding, product descriptions | Functional — explains what it does |
| **Flock Together** | Marketing, social media, landing page hero, App Store description | Brand rallying cry — the hook |
| **Together Together** | Campaigns, merch, emotional moments, launch video | Vibes — the feeling |

## Color Palette
- **Flamingo Pink:** #FF6B81 (primary — icon, buttons, accents)
- **Light Pink:** #FFF0F3 (backgrounds, light surfaces)
- **White:** #FFFFFF (text on pink backgrounds)
- **Dark:** #1A1A2E (text on light backgrounds, optional dark mode)

## Typography Direction
- Headings: Luxury condensed serif (tall, confident, wide letter-spacing)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (system fonts in-app)
- Taglines: Small caps, spaced out, lighter weight

## Icon Mark (in progress)
- Direction 1: Petal burst (6-petal flower shape) in oval frame
- Direction 2: Abstract spiral/circular lines that read as flamingo
- Direction 3: Flowing line flamingo silhouette
- Direction 4: Dual flamingo necks forming heart/infinity

## Positioning
- Primary audience: Women 25-40 who organize social gatherings
- Tone: Confident, social, feminine-leaning, not cutesy
- Flamingo = social by nature, vibrant, unmistakable
- Pink = owned confidently, not apologetically
